# Ink to Structure

Selected by Monty: the first mixed concept, Ink to Structure.

Warm white, near-black, Hanken Grotesk at weight 800, compact supporting typography and thin rules. Actual owned photographs become printed halftone dots, contour traces and gently drifting particles. No invented portraits, nostalgic engraving, gradients or ornamental scenery.

The opening uses the Teton hike as a full-screen photograph. Native scroll transforms the actual image pixels through halftone, contours and particles. Building has a monochrome connected systems composition anchored by the real Prometheus project. Writing has a geometric two-roller press feeding toward the real essay list. The existing Things I Love pinboard remains intact.

Ambient motion is small and slow, paused outside the viewport and when the document is hidden. Scroll drives the press crank and sheet. Each artwork has a pause control; reduced motion keeps the original photograph and static artwork. Links and essay titles are ordinary accessible HTML, not painted into artwork.

## Finished artwork revision

The visual reference is the approved “01 / Ink to Structure” concept. Production uses finished raster illustrations, preserving detailed mechanical shading and the solid-to-wireframe composition. Asset locations: `public/artwork/ink-press.webp`, `ink-systems.webp`, and `ink-landscape.webp`. They were generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the approved concept as reference, then encoded as WebP. The landscape is an artistic derivative of the supplied Teton photo; the actual photo remains the opening and reduced-motion fallback.

Asset prompts: press only, transparent background, rich black halftone at left blending into exact fine construction lines at right, blank opaque paper, no baked-in text; systems only, near-black wide banner with white dimensional halftone spheres transitioning into schematic outlines; landscape, preserve supplied hiker and terrain composition while replacing sky with warm white and transitioning photographic detail into halftone and contours. Full prompt specifications are archived with the artwork in `public/artwork/README.md`.

Composition: mobile header and oversized black name on warm white precede the photo. Scroll crossfades into the finished landscape art. Large artwork replaces primitive SVG machinery. The printed paper carries the newest actual essay as a normal HTML link, followed by a clear editorial archive. Particles are sampled from the ink rather than unrelated random points. Reduced motion and offscreen/hidden tab suspension remain required. Pinboard is untouched.
